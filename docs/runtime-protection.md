# PostgreSQL serving credential boundary

Provision two distinct authenticated `LOGIN INHERIT` roles with `scripts/provision-runtime-roles.sql`. Each is a direct member of exactly one designated application role (`charter_workspace_app` or `charter_evidence_app`), with inherited permissions and no admin option. Application groups are `NOLOGIN` leaf roles with no memberships. Any other membership is rejected, even if it currently grants no inherited privileges or cannot currently be assumed. Granting the intended leaf role with `SET` allowed is safe only because the login inherits all its relevant ACLs and the leaf's own privileged attributes and DDL authorities are checked.

Startup verifies the expected authenticated login against both `session_user` and `current_user`, on one PostgreSQL connection and authorization snapshot. A startup option or pool hook must not switch role or session authorization. Runtime roles may not own the evidence database, evidence schema, or evidence relations, or be members of their owners; they may not create schemas in the evidence database or objects in the evidence schema. Do not grant application `SECURITY DEFINER` execution to these roles. The evidence writer has table-wide `SELECT` and `INSERT` on evidence and no grant options; separate workspace tables are inaccessible to it. The workspace credential gets no evidence table or column privileges. PostgreSQL administrator and migration credentials are kept out of the serving process.

This is a startup configuration check, not a defense against a trusted PostgreSQL administrator changing privileges or binaries during service. Restart and recheck before using changed credential, ACL, schema, or group configurations. PostgreSQL append-only permissions and trigger guards are not a retention/WORM boundary for an owner or administrator.
